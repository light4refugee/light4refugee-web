Three Steps. That's All It Takes.

1

Choose Your Method

GoFundMe for card and online payments worldwide. MTN MoMo for Rwanda. WorldRemit or Revolut for diaspora sending internationally to Rwanda.

2

Send Your Gift

Any amount — even a small contribution funds a week of materials for a refugee student. There is no minimum. Every gift matters and is used directly.

3

We Confirm & Report

GoFundMe sends automatic receipts. For MoMo and WorldRemit, email us at info@light4refugee.org and we'll send a personal acknowledgement and our next impact report.

We want to be honest with you. L4R is a growing nonprofit — we are currently working on registering our formal payment gateway so we can accept online card payments directly on this site. Until then, GoFundMe is the most secure and receipted way to give internationally, and MTN MoMo / WorldRemit are the fastest routes for Rwanda-based and diaspora donors. Every franc and every dollar goes directly to our programs — no admin bloat, no middlemen.

Questions? Write to us at info@light4refugee.org or WhatsApp +250 792 052 725 and we'll respond within 24 hours.
