Application Tips

How to Win the Scholarships You Deserve

Four things that separate successful refugee scholarship applications from unsuccessful ones — from our College Counseling team.

01

Start Early — Months, Not Weeks

Most strong scholarship applications take 3–6 months to prepare. Start researching in September for scholarships with July deadlines. Use this Hub as your calendar.

02

Your Story is Your Strength

Scholarship panels are not just looking for grades — they are looking for people. Write a personal statement that tells your specific story with honesty, precision, and purpose. L4R's ECP helps you build this skill.

03

Document Everything Now

UNHCR registration, academic transcripts, refugee ID, references — gather every document you need before you need it. Missing documents have cost students scholarship offers at the final stage.

04

Apply to Multiple — Strategically

Never apply to just one scholarship. Build a portfolio: 2–3 Rwanda options, 1–2 African, 1 global reach. L4R's College Counseling helps you prioritize based on your profile and the deadlines that overlap.

Ready to Apply? Let's Build Your Application Together.

Every scholarship on this page becomes more within reach with strong English, a compelling personal statement, and the right guidance. L4R's College Counseling, ECP, and ISEP programs exist exactly for this — and they are all free.
