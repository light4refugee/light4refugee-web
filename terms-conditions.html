2 About Light4Refugee

Light 4 Refugee (L4R) is a refugee-led nonprofit initiative dedicated to empowering refugee youth through academic preparation, college counseling, mentorship, career guidance, and leadership development.

Our programs operate primarily within refugee camps in Rwanda — including Mahama, Kigeme, and Mugombwa — and extend to refugee communities across the region. All programs are offered free of charge to eligible students.
