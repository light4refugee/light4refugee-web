Talent Hub

Trained, motivated young people and skilled refugees in Rwanda, introduced to employers by people who know them. Consent-based, free of charge, and handled with care from the first message to the first day at work.

Why employers use Talent Hub

One contact. Prepared candidates. No surprises.

Prepared by L4R. Candidates come through our programs, career guidance and English training.
Introduced with consent. A candidate is introduced only after saying yes to your opportunity.
Free to use. We charge no fee to employers or to candidates.
We stay involved. We follow up with you and the candidate in the first months.
Two lanes, one clear purpose

Opportunities close to home, and pathways abroad

Light4Refugee focuses on local and regional opportunities, where we can introduce, support and follow up. International pathways are led by UNHCR and its partners, and we prepare candidates for them.

Led by Light4Refugee

Local & Regional Opportunities

Internships, jobs and short-term work in Rwanda, and in other East African countries where the law and the employer allow it.

  • Anonymous shortlist first, introduction only with the candidate's yes
  • Employers verified before they receive any candidate information
  • Follow-up with employer and candidate after placement

How it works

Five steps, with a person on both sides

Nothing is automatic. A real member of our team reviews each request, and each candidate decides for themselves.

1

Tell us what you need

Send the request form below with the role, skills and place.

2

We get to know you

We check your organisation and share our Talent Partner Agreement for signing.

3

Anonymous shortlist

You see skills, education and language level. No names or contacts yet.

4

The candidate says yes

We introduce only candidates who agree to your opportunity.

5

You interview and decide

You choose, then we check in with you and the candidate over the first months.

Safe for everyone

Built on consent and fair work

Refugees are often treated as numbers. Talent Hub is designed so that they are treated as people who choose.

For candidates

  • You choose. Joining is optional, and you say yes or no to every opportunity.
  • You stay private. Employers first see only your skills. Your name, camp and contacts stay hidden until you agree.
  • You never pay. Nobody may charge you to be considered, introduced or hired.
  • You can leave any time without losing any support from L4R.

For employers

  • Fair pay and safe work. A written offer, fair pay or stipend, and a safe workplace.
  • Adults only. Candidates are 18 or older.
  • Data respected. Candidate information is used only for your opportunity and deleted if there is no hire.
  • Honest follow-up. Short check-ins in the first months, so every placement has a chance to succeed.
Want to do more? Employers who wish to may also support our scholars, for example by sponsoring a place, offering mentors or hosting interns. This is always optional and never affects who we introduce.
