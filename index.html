Light4Refugee — Our Story
About L4R

Join Hands, Change a Life That Changes Many

Light4Refugee is a refugee-led nonprofit operating inside Rwanda's refugee camps — Mahama and Kiziba (Soon expanding to other Camps). We equip refugee youth with the academic preparation, college counseling, and career guidance they need to move from surviving to leading. 100% of our team has lived the experience we serve.

Our Drive

To empower refugee youth through academic preparation, mentorship, and career guidance — increasing their access to higher education, building confidence, and nurturing the next generation of leaders.

Free programs for all eligible students
Operating across 2 refugee camps in Rwanda
100% refugee-founded and refugee-led

Your donation keeps every L4R program free for every eligible student — no exceptions, no exclusions.

Our Vision — In Full

We envision a world where displacement does not define destiny. Refugee students have the learning support, mentorship, and opportunities they need to grow into confident leaders who lift their families, communities, and countries — in Rwanda and far beyond.

We are igniting the potential of refugee youth — equipping them with education, mentorship, and meaningful opportunities to become resilient, confident, and influential leaders who reshape their future and contribute to the renewal of their communities.

Our Mission

Rise, Learn, and Thrive — Through Preparation, Guidance & Mentorship

We empower refugee youth to access higher education and build the future they deserve.

See full version

Our Mission — In Full

Through academic preparation, career guidance, and mentorship, we empower refugee youth to rise, learn, and thrive.

Light4Refugee equips refugee youth with the tools and guidance to access higher education, build employable skills, and lead change — through targeted academic support, college counseling, and career guidance that increase their chances to succeed in institutions and workplaces around the world.

Value 01

Empowerment

Equipping refugees with tools to take control of their future.

Flip to learn more

Empowerment

We believe every refugee youth carries potential that no displacement can erase. Our role is to equip them — not to do things for them, but to give them the tools, confidence, and knowledge to drive their own future. True empowerment means they graduate from our programs no longer needing us.

Value 02

Resilience

We celebrate the strength it takes to rise from adversity.

Flip to learn more

Resilience

Refugee youth have already demonstrated extraordinary resilience just by surviving and showing up. We recognize and celebrate that strength — building on it rather than treating adversity as a deficit. Our programs are designed to channel resilience into academic achievement, professional readiness, and community leadership.

Value 03

Accountability

We take full responsibility for our promises, actions, and results.

Flip to learn more

Accountability

We hold ourselves to the highest standard — not because donors are watching, but because the students we serve deserve nothing less. We track outcomes, report transparently, and measure what matters: how many students access higher education, build careers, and carry their communities forward.

Value 04

Leadership

We nurture future changemakers who will rebuild their communities.

Flip to learn more

Leadership

We don't just want to help students graduate — we want to develop leaders. Every session, every mentorship call, and every bootcamp day is designed to build the leadership capacity that helps a student become someone who uplifts others. Today's refugee learner is tomorrow's community builder.
