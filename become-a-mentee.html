What You'll Gain

Everything You Need to Go Further

As an L4R mentee, you get access to programs, mentors, and a community that believes in your potential unconditionally.

Intensive Academic Preparation

Sharpen your English language skills and critical thinking through a rigorous, refugee-informed curriculum designed to close the gap between your potential and your opportunities.

Personalised College Counseling

One-on-one guidance through university applications, personal statement writing, scholarship searches, and recommendation letters — so no door closes simply because you didn't know how to knock.

Career Guidance & Mentorship

Be matched with a professional mentor who shares your field of interest. Build a clear, confident path from school to career — with CV coaching, mock interviews, and real-world professional advice.

A Vibrant Global Network

Connect with fellow refugee students, international mentors, partner organisations, and alumni who have walked your path and are now changing the world — from Kigali to London to New York.

Free, Fully Supported Programs

Every L4R program is 100% free to eligible students. No hidden fees, no financial barriers. Your only investment is your commitment, curiosity, and willingness to grow.

A Community That Sees You

At L4R, you're not a statistic. You're a person with a story, potential, and a future worth investing in. Every mentor, every session, every program is built around you thriving — not just surviving.

Who Can Apply

Are You Eligible to Become an L4R Mentee?

Our programs are designed specifically for refugee students in Rwanda. We work hard to make sure the right students get the right support at the right time.

You must have refugee status in Rwanda
For Academic Prep & College Counseling: Senior 4 (S4) secondary students
For Career Guidance & Lighting Paths: current university students
Willing to commit to sessions and engage actively in the program
Access to a phone or device that can connect to the internet (for online sessions)
Resident in one of Rwanda's refugee camps or urban refugee communities

Don't perfectly match every criterion? Apply anyway when applications open — we assess every student holistically and with empathy. What matters most is your commitment and your desire to grow.

How It Works

From Application to Graduation — Your Journey

01

Watch for Announcements

Follow us on Facebook and Instagram, or add your email below. We announce new cohort openings on our website and social channels first.

02

Submit Your Application

When applications open, fill out the Google Form with your background, goals, and which program you're applying for. The form is straightforward and takes about 10 minutes.

03

Selection & Notification

Our team reviews every application personally. Selected students are notified by email and phone within 2 weeks of the application deadline.

04

Onboarding & Matching

You'll be welcomed into your cohort, matched with a mentor, and briefed on your program schedule, expectations, and resources.

05

Learn, Grow & Shine

Attend sessions, engage with your mentor, build skills, and take steps toward your academic and career goals — one week at a time.

Mentee Questions — Answered

Are L4R programs really free? +
Yes — 100% free. All L4R programs are completely free to eligible and selected refugee students. We believe financial barriers should never stand in the way of education and opportunity.
I don't have reliable internet — can I still participate? +
We do our best to accommodate students with limited connectivity. Our Field Week sessions are in-person, which helps. For online sessions, we work with mentees on scheduling and format to make participation as accessible as possible. Reach out to us and we'll figure it out together.
What if applications are closed when I check? +
Add your email to our notification list on this page. When a new cohort opens — whether it's Cohort 2, the Career Lighting Paths programme, or the College Readiness Bootcamp — you'll be among the very first to know.
I'm not at S4 level — can I still apply? +
Different programs serve different levels. The Career Lighting Paths programme is for current university students. Keep an eye on upcoming cohort announcements — we plan to expand our programs over time and criteria may vary per cohort.
How long do programs run? +
It varies by program. The Academic Prep Field Week is 5 days in-person followed by ongoing weekly online mentoring. The Career Lighting Paths programme runs for 11 weeks. The College Readiness Bootcamp duration will be announced when launched.
