Light 4 Refugee is a refugee-led organisation operating in Mahama & Kiziba refugee camps in Rwanda. We believe education is the most reliable path to a dignified, self-reliant future.

"For many, becoming a refugee feels like the end. But with a second chance, they can rewrite their story." — L4R
01
Academic Preparation

📘 Unlocking Opportunity Through Language

Closing the English gap that stands between talent and opportunity.
"Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world." — Nelson Mandela

Refugee students graduating from high school in Rwanda often show extraordinary academic potential. But a critical barrier stands in their way: English proficiency. Due to disrupted schooling and limited resources inside refugee settlements, many face serious challenges mastering English — a non-negotiable skill for higher education and global competition.

The numbers tell the story. According to UNHCR, while 70% of refugee youth complete secondary school, only 4% go on to access tertiary education. The language gap, compounded by a shortage of scholarship opportunities, makes that statistic painfully real.

Our Response

Rigorous English immersion programme tailored to refugee learners
Intensive language training with ongoing academic challenges
Local & global scholarship preparation support
Personalised academic growth tracking and mentorship

but only 4% reach university

02
College Counseling

🎓 Guiding Potential to Possibility

Because refugee voices deserve a place in lecture halls across the world.
"Refugee voices deserve a place in lecture halls across the world."

For refugees in Rwanda, protracted displacement is the norm. Camps like Kiziba have existed since 1996 and continue receiving new arrivals. With hopes of return diminishing, education becomes the most reliable path to a dignified, self-reliant future.

Yet from the 70% of refugee youth who complete high school, only 4% access higher education. Financial hardship, limited support systems, and unfamiliarity with the college application process all stand in the way.

Our Response

Coaching on writing compelling college essays and personal statements
Guidance on application forms, deadlines & scholarship portals
Supporting recommendation letter requests from teachers
Interview preparation and scholarship awareness campaigns
4% of refugee youth access
tertiary education — we change that

03
Career Guidance

💼 From Graduation to Vocation

We're not just preparing students for jobs — we're preparing them for leadership.
"We're not just preparing students for jobs — we're preparing them for leadership."

Many refugee university graduates in Rwanda display incredible resilience and talent — yet a gap remains: a lack of professional readiness. Graduates face uncertainty about career paths, limited guidance, and scarce resources to break into the workforce.

Rwanda's unemployment rate stood at 24.3% in 2022 (UNHCR), with opportunities especially scarce for refugees. Many end up returning to camps after graduation, unable to leverage their hard-earned degrees. Our 11-week programme changes that.

Our 11-Week Programme Equips Students With

Career mapping and professional pathway planning
Resume, cover letter writing and LinkedIn profile building
Professional communication & soft skills training
Mock interviews, networking strategies and job placement
