Become a Mentor

Be the Guiding Light
for a Refugee Youth

At Light4Refugee, mentorship isn't just about giving advice — it's about walking alongside someone as they rebuild their future. Your hour a week can reshape an entire life.

What You Give

1–2 hours per week of your time
Your expertise, experience & encouragement
Reliable internet access for virtual sessions
A passion for equity, youth & education

Join a growing network of mentors helping refugee youth in Rwanda unlock their potential — one conversation at a time.

Apply Now — It's Free
Why Mentor With Us

Make a Direct Impact on a Refugee Student's Life

Every mentor who joins Light4Refugee becomes part of something bigger than a weekly call. You become a proof of possibility — showing a young refugee that the world beyond the camp sees them, believes in them, and is willing to invest in their future.

Direct, Personal Impact

You're not donating to a fund — you're speaking directly to a student who needs your voice. Your guidance shapes real decisions.

Share Your Career Insight

Industry experience, career tips, interview coaching — knowledge that took you years to build can fast-track a student's entire trajectory.

Join a Community of Changemakers

Connect with a global network of educators, professionals, and advocates committed to equity and refugee empowerment.

Mentorship Areas

Academic Support Career Guidance College Counseling Personal Mentorship

What We're Looking For

Passion for education, youth or refugee empowerment
Students, professionals or educators — any background welcome
Ability to commit 1–2 hours per week consistently
Good communication skills and patience
Reliable internet access for virtual mentorship sessions
