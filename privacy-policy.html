Privacy Policy

At Light 4 Refugee, protecting the dignity, safety, and personal information of the communities we serve is part of our mission. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard information shared through our website and programs.

Effective Date: August 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all visitors, volunteers, partners, donors, students, mentors, and supporters interacting with Light 4 Refugee through our website, forms, communications, and digital platforms.

Quick Navigation

1 Who We Are

Light 4 Refugee (L4R) is a refugee-led nonprofit initiative committed to empowering refugee youth through academic preparation, college counseling, mentorship, leadership development, and career guidance.

Our website and online platforms are designed to provide information about our programs, connect with supporters, and create educational opportunities for displaced youth across Rwanda and beyond.

2 Information We Collect

We may collect information voluntarily shared with us as well as limited technical information automatically gathered through your use of our website.

  • Name, email address, and contact details
  • Volunteer, mentorship, or scholarship applications
  • Messages submitted through contact forms
  • Newsletter subscriptions
  • Donation-related information processed through secure third-party platforms
  • Basic technical information such as browser type, IP address, and pages visited

Important: We do not knowingly collect unnecessary personal data and we never sell personal information to advertisers or third parties.

3 How We Use Your Information

Information shared with Light 4 Refugee is used responsibly and only for purposes connected to our mission and operations.

  • Responding to inquiries and communication requests
  • Managing educational programs and mentorship opportunities
  • Sending newsletters, updates, and announcements
  • Improving the functionality and accessibility of our website
  • Coordinating volunteers, partners, and community events
  • Maintaining organizational accountability and security

4 Sharing Information

Light 4 Refugee values confidentiality and limits information sharing to situations where it is operationally necessary or legally required.

  • Trusted service providers supporting our website or communication systems
  • Donation and payment platforms with secure processing systems
  • Partner organizations where consent has been provided
  • Authorities where disclosure is legally required

Any third-party organization working with Light 4 Refugee is expected to maintain appropriate privacy and data protection standards.

5 Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical precautions to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, disclosure, or loss.

While we work to maintain secure systems, no digital platform or internet transmission method can guarantee complete security.

Security Reminder: Users should avoid sending sensitive personal information through unsecured email or public networks whenever possible.

6 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve performance, understand visitor engagement, and enhance user experience.

  • Remembering user preferences
  • Analyzing website traffic and performance
  • Improving navigation and accessibility

You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features of the site may not function properly afterward.

7 Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights concerning your personal information.

  • Request access to information we hold about you
  • Request corrections or updates to inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent from communication subscriptions
  • Request clarification on how your data is used

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

8 Children's Privacy

Protecting young people is central to our work. Our website is not intended to collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without appropriate supervision or consent.

If we become aware that information from a child has been collected improperly, we will take reasonable steps to remove it promptly.

9 Changes to This Policy

Light 4 Refugee may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect operational, legal, or technological changes.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of our website indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

10 Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests related to this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us:

  • Email: info@light4refugee.org
  • Location: Mahama Refugee Camp, Kirehe District, Rwanda
  • Website: light4refugee.org

We aim to respond to all reasonable inquiries within 48 business hours.

Your Trust Matters to Us

Light 4 Refugee believes that transparency and accountability are essential to building meaningful relationships with students, volunteers, partners, and supporters around the world.
