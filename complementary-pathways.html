What This Program Is

A Database That Puts Skilled Refugees in the Right Room When the Right Door Opens

Refugees living in camps across Rwanda often carry impressive educational backgrounds, professional qualifications, and practical skills — built before displacement and often quietly maintained after. The problem isn't the talent. It's visibility.

When organizations — including UNHCR Rwanda, NGOs, businesses, and programs — look for candidates with specific expertise, refugee communities are frequently missed. Not because they don't qualify, but because no organized, accessible record of their skills exists.

Complementary Pathways exists to change that. We collect, verify where possible, and organize the skills and qualifications of refugees in our network — then work with UNHCR Rwanda to ensure that when real opportunities emerge, the right people are already documented and reachable.

What We Want to Be Clear About

Registering with Complementary Pathways does not guarantee employment, a scholarship, a position, or any specific outcome. We are not a placement agency. What we do is maintain a credible, organized record of skills so that you are not invisible when opportunities your qualifications match become available. We notify — we do not decide. Decisions rest with UNHCR and the organizations they work with.

How It Works
01

You Submit Your Profile

In person at our Mahama office, or online through the form on this page. You share your qualifications, skills, languages, and any supporting documents you have.

02

L4R Reviews and Records

Our team reviews each submission. We organize profiles by field and skill type, note what documentation exists, and flag where verification may be needed. Accuracy matters more than volume.

03

Shared with UNHCR Rwanda

Aggregated, organized skill data is shared with UNHCR Rwanda. Where individual profiles are relevant to a specific opportunity, they are surfaced through UNHCR's coordination channels — always with appropriate data handling protocols.

04

When an Opportunity Matches You

If a relevant opportunity emerges and your profile matches, we or UNHCR will reach out through your registered contact. You are never submitted anywhere without your awareness.

05

Verification Along the Way

For qualifications that cannot be immediately verified (e.g., documents lost during displacement), you note the issuing institution. Background and credential verification happens as part of the opportunity process — not as a barrier to registering.

Register Your Skills

You're More Than What Displacement Left Behind

Whether you come in person to our Mahama office or complete this form online — your skills deserve to be on record. Registration is free, takes about 10 minutes, and keeps your profile in a system watched by people who may one day have exactly the right opportunity for you.

Open to all refugees in Rwanda

Mahama, Kiziba, Kigeme, Mugombwa, Nyabiheke, and urban refugees in Kigali — all welcome.

All education levels

Degree, diploma, certificate, vocational training, or self-taught practical skills — all are valuable and all are recorded.

Prefer in-person?

Walk into our office at Mahama Refugee Camp. Our team will complete the form with you directly.

Your Privacy Is Protected

Personal details — including your UNHCR case number, phone number, and nationality — are stored securely and never displayed publicly. They are accessed only by the L4R team and shared with UNHCR Rwanda in accordance with established data protection protocols. You may request to update or remove your profile at any time by contacting complementary-pathways@light4refugee.org.

Common Questions

A few things people often ask before registering.

Will registering guarantee me a job or opportunity?
No — and we want to be honest about that. Complementary Pathways is a skills database, not a placement service. What it does is make sure your qualifications are on record and organized, so that when real, relevant opportunities emerge through UNHCR or its partners, your name is part of the conversation. We surface candidates — we do not control decisions.
What if I lost my certificates during displacement?
Still register. Many refugees lost documents when they were forced to flee — that is a reality, not a disqualification. In your form, name the institution where you studied, the country, and the approximate year. Verification is a process that happens over time, not a barrier to being included in the database today.
Who will see my personal information?
Your personal details — including your phone number, case number, and nationality — are stored securely and only accessible to the L4R team. Aggregated, organised skill data is shared with UNHCR Rwanda for program coordination. Individual profiles are only shared when directly relevant to a specific opportunity, and always in accordance with data protection protocols. Your profile will never appear publicly on this website.
I don't live in Mahama. Can I still register?
Absolutely. The online form on this page works for any refugee living anywhere in Rwanda — Kiziba, Kigeme, Mugombwa, Nyabiheke, urban Kigali, or elsewhere. The Mahama office is our base of operations, but the database is open to all.
Can I update or remove my profile later?
Yes. Email us at complementary-pathways@light4refugee.org with your name and the changes you'd like made. We'll update your profile within 5 business days. If you want your profile removed entirely, we'll do that too — no questions asked.
What types of opportunities might this lead to?
This varies and we cannot predict specific outcomes. UNHCR Rwanda and its partner organizations work across health, education, livelihoods, social services, technical fields, and more. When their work requires specific expertise and qualified candidates are needed, the database is one of the resources they consult. The scope of what might arise is broad — which is exactly why capturing a wide range of skills matters.
