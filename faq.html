About Light 4 Refugee

Who we are, where we work, and what makes us different

Light4Refugee (L4R) is a refugee youth-led organisation dedicated to empowering refugee students through academic preparation, university accessibility, and career guidance. Our mission is to break the cycle of poverty — often resulting from displacement — by empowering refugee youth to access quality education, build sustainable careers, and live lives of dignity.

We operate from the conviction that true and lasting change always comes from within: our team is made up of refugees who have lived the experience and are committed to building the path for those who follow.

L4R currently operates in Rwanda, serving students from different refugee camps — mainly Mahama and Kiziba Refugee Camps — as well as urban refugee communities. We are actively working to stretch our impact to all refugee camps across Rwanda.

L4R was founded by young refugees who witnessed firsthand the urgency of targeted interventions for sustainable transformation in refugee communities. Our lived experiences inspired a grassroots solution designed to bridge the critical educational and opportunity gaps that hinder the potential of refugee youth.

We work from the premise that "true and lasting change always comes from within."

L4R is developed by refugees, for refugees. What truly sets our programs apart is a contextualized, blended learning model that:

  • Integrates academic excellence with targeted mentorship
  • Builds practical, real-world skills alongside classroom knowledge
  • Is informed by a profound, firsthand understanding of the barriers refugee students encounter
  • Delivers holistic, sustained support — not just short-term fixes

We empower students not just to succeed, but to emerge as leaders.

Light4Refugee envisions a world where displacement does not define destiny. We see a future where refugee youth are not only accessing transformative educational opportunities, but are empowered to become confident leaders, innovators, and trailblazers who actively shape their own lives and enrich their host communities.

Our three commitments for the next five years:

  • Expand Our Reach: Bring our proven programs to more refugee camps and urban communities across Rwanda.
  • Elevate Student Outcomes: Increase the number of students securing prestigious scholarships locally and internationally and transitioning into meaningful employment.
  • Forge Powerful Alliances: Cultivate strategic partnerships with educational institutions, employers, and support organisations to provide students with essential resources and networks.

Our Programs

Eligibility, applications, scholarships and what to expect

L4R provides a comprehensive range of support across three core programs:

  • Academic Preparation: Intensive English language training to close the proficiency gap that stands between talent and university access.
  • College Counseling: Personalised guidance on college essays, scholarship applications, recommendation letters, and interview preparation.
  • Career Guidance: An 11-week professional development programme covering career mapping, CV writing, soft skills, mock interviews, and job placement support.

Yes — 100%. All L4R programs are completely free to eligible and selected refugee students. We believe financial barriers should never stand in the way of education and opportunity.

Students can apply by filling out an application form when recruitment is announced. Details are shared on our Applications Hub, social media channels, and through community channels inside the camps.

💡 Tip: Follow us on Facebook and Instagram to be the first to know when applications open for new cohorts.

While specific eligibility criteria can vary for each L4R program, the following general requirements apply:

  • Most applicants must have a refugee background.
  • For Academic Preparation and College Counseling programs, applicants typically need to be Senior Four students.
  • For the Career Guidance program, applicants should be current university students.
📋 Check the detailed requirements when specific program applications are announced via our website — criteria may vary per cohort.

While college placement is not guaranteed, our team provides everything a student needs to submit their strongest possible application.

Support & Partnership

Donations, partnerships, and how to stay connected

We welcome partnerships that support our mission through funding, resources, mentorship, or expertise. Reach out to us via info@light4refugee.org or through the Partner With Us page on our website.

Donations go directly toward supporting our programs, including:

  • Curriculum materials and learning resources
  • Internet access for students inside camps
  • Student support and welfare during programs
  • Training sessions and workshops
  • Staff development and capacity building

We are committed to full transparency about how every dollar is used.

We share updates through newsletters, social media stories, and our annual impact report — so you can see directly how your generosity changes lives. Every student story, every scholarship won, every career breakthrough is tracked and shared with our community of supporters.

Follow us and stay connected with our work and students on:

  • Facebook — Light 4 Refugee
  • Instagram — @light4refugee
  • YouTube Channel
  • LinkedIn — Light 4 Refugee

You can reach us at info@light4refugee.org or through the contact form on our website at light4refugee.org/contact-us. We aim to respond within 48 hours.

Our Vision

Where We're Headed in the Next 5 Years
